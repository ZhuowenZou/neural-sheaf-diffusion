\section{A Faithful Event-Time Implementation of the Temporal Core}
\label{app:faithful}

The released implementation realizes the temporal core of
Section~3.2 as an \emph{efficient approximation}: at every snapshot it
feeds a length-two pseudo-sequence (a context token and a current
token) through an off-the-shelf selective-SSM (Mamba) block, and
recurrence across events is carried by cached embeddings rather than
by the SSM state itself. Consequently the physical inter-event gap
$\Delta_k$ never enters the computation, and the selective-scan
recurrence never spans more than two tokens. During the discussion
period we additionally implemented the algorithm of Section~3.2
\emph{as written} and benchmarked it under identical protocols. This
appendix documents the differences, the modifications that proved
necessary to train it stably, and its empirical standing.

\subsection{Architectural differences}
\label{app:faithful:arch}

\paragraph{Persistent per-node state with exact ZOH.}
Each node $u$ carries a memory $h_u \in \mathbb{R}^{d_h}$ that
persists across the entire event stream. On event $k$ the state is
advanced by the \emph{exact} zero-order-hold discretization of the
continuous system $\dot h = A h + B_{u,k}\, q_{u,k}$,
\begin{equation}
h_{u,k} \;=\; e^{\Delta_{u,k} A}\, h_{u,k-1}
\;+\; A^{-1}\!\left(e^{\Delta_{u,k} A} - I\right) B_{u,k}\, q_{u,k},
\label{eq:app-zoh}
\end{equation}
computed with a batched matrix exponential and a batched linear
solve --- no scan approximation and no diagonal restriction of $A$.

\paragraph{Physical time in the step-size selector.}
The paper introduces $\Delta_k$ in Eq.~(11) but omits it from the
selector input in Eq.~(15). We resolve this in favor of Eq.~(11): the
per-node step size is
\begin{equation}
\Delta_{u,k} \;=\;
\min\!\Big(\operatorname{softplus}\big(w_\Delta^\top q_{u,k} + b_\Delta
+ \gamma \log\!\big(1 + \Delta_k / s\big)\big),\, \Delta_{\max}\Big),
\end{equation}
with learnable $\gamma, s$, so the effective integration step is
conditioned on both content and the physical inter-event gap. The cap
$\Delta_{\max}$ controls transient amplification of the non-normal
generator. $B_{u,k}=\mathrm{reshape}(W_B q_{u,k}+b_B)$ is
input-selective with its bias initialized from the HiPPO-LegS $B$.

\paragraph{Provably stable generator (necessary modification).}
$A$ is initialized from HiPPO-LegS. Left unconstrained, training
drifts $A$ out of the Hurwitz region; because
\eqref{eq:app-zoh} is applied thousands of times along an event
stream, any unstable mode compounds geometrically (we measured state
magnitudes growing from $10^{-2}$ to $10^{20}$ within $2{,}400$
events before overflow). Exploiting that HiPPO-LegS is lower
triangular with diagonal $-(i{+}1)$, we parameterize
\begin{equation}
A \;=\; \mathrm{tril}_{-1}(L) \;-\;
\operatorname{diag}\big(\operatorname{softplus}(a)\big),
\end{equation}
which reproduces the HiPPO initialization exactly and guarantees
$\operatorname{Re}\,\lambda_i(A) < 0$ for \emph{every} parameter
value --- the same stability principle enforced by diagonal
parameterizations in S4/Mamba, lifted to the full triangular
structure. On streams with $\sim$$10^5$ events this modification is
the difference between convergence and silent divergence.

\paragraph{Causal input assembly.}
The SSM input is $q_{u,k} = [\,x_u(t_k);\, \bar z_u(t_{k-1});\,
\psi_u(G_k)\,]$, where $\bar z$ is the mean-aggregated spatial
readout over the \emph{previous} event graph (the lagged feedback of
Eq.~(13), preserving causality) and $\psi_u$ contains degree
descriptors of the current event graph.

\paragraph{Memory readout.}
The readout map $C$ of Eq.~(5) (Table~3), which the efficient
implementation never consumes, is realized as a normalization of the
memory ($o_u = \mathrm{LN}(h_u)$) applied before all consumers. Its
effect is protocol-dependent: neutral-to-harmful when the recurrence
is short, and decisively beneficial (+0.03 NDCG on
\textsc{tgbn-trade}) once training streams are long enough to train
the recurrence.

\paragraph{Event-locked sheaf decoding and spatial integration.}
Restriction maps are decoded \emph{once per event} from the memory
readout (orthogonal maps via Householder composition; normalized
sheaf connection Laplacian $\tilde L$) and held fixed over the
inter-event interval. The spatial latent is initialized as
$z_u(t_k^+) = P_z[\,x_u; o_u\,]$ and evolved by forward-Euler steps
of the diffusion field of Eq.~(23),
\begin{equation}
Z \leftarrow Z + \tau_\ell\,
\sigma\big((I - \tilde L)(I \otimes W_1)\, Z\, W_2\big),
\qquad \tau_\ell = \operatorname{softplus}(\theta_\ell),
\end{equation}
with a single tied $(W_1, W_2)$, interpreting Eq.~(23) as a vector
field rather than a discrete layer map (empirically superior and
consistent with the continuous-time narrative of Section~3.1).

\paragraph{Scalability.}
The batched matrix exponential materializes an
$(n, d_h, d_h)$ tensor; on million-node graphs we compute
\eqref{eq:app-zoh} in node chunks of $32{,}768$ under gradient
checkpointing. This trains end-to-end on \textsc{tkgl-wikidata}
(1.23M entities, 19.7M edges) within 7.6\,GB of GPU memory.

\paragraph{Training protocol.}
Two safeguards matter for correctness of reported numbers on long
streams: (i) gradient clipping plus a non-finite-gradient step skip,
and (ii) model selection that requires a \emph{finite validation
loss} alongside the best validation metric --- a diverged model
scores NaN on all candidates, which ranking evaluators score as a
perfect MRR. Early stopping must be patient: faithful training
exhibits transient degradation followed by slow recovery (best epochs
up to 150 on \textsc{tgbn-trade}).

\subsection{Evaluation protocol and its corrections}
\label{app:faithful:protocol}

All numbers in this appendix use the official TGB splits, negative sets and
evaluators, with the metric read \emph{by name} from the evaluator output
(TGB's link evaluator returns Hits@10 and MRR together). An audit of our
harness against the TGB reference scripts found, and fixed, three defects
that had inflated earlier drafts; we state them because they affect any
snapshot-based harness.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Predict-then-update.} Scoring the events of snapshot $k$ from
embeddings diffused over snapshot $k$ itself leaks the events being
predicted. We now score snapshot $k$ from the state produced by snapshot
$k{-}1$ (with the gradient attached during training) and ingest snapshot
$k$ afterwards, mirroring the batch-wise predict-then-update loop of the
TGB reference models. A random-destination probe (no learnable signal)
scores at chance under this protocol and far above chance under the old one.
\item \emph{Label cursor.} TGB's node-label iterator is monotone across
train$\to$val$\to$test; resetting it per split paired validation/test
snapshots with the earliest labels. The cursor is now seeked to the
continuous position.
\item \emph{Units of $\Delta_k$.} On second-resolution streams the raw gap
saturated the step-size selector at its cap; $\Delta_k$ is now measured in
units of the median training inter-snapshot gap.
\end{enumerate}
Two further guards: non-finite scores are ranked last (the TGB evaluator
would rank a NaN first), and NDCG is averaged per label timestamp as in the
TGB examples.

\paragraph{Head components (documented separately from the core).}
The temporal core is unchanged across datasets; task-appropriate head
components are attached and attributed individually: a recurrency decoder
(REC-x; event-exact causal counts/recency of $(s,r,o)$, untyped $(s,o)$ and
symmetric channels feeding a zero-initialised bonus MLP), relation-aware
input (REL), node-type embeddings (TYPE), and learnable node embeddings
(EMB, optionally also visible to the scoring head).

\paragraph{What the components contribute (leak-free ablations).}
We ablate the three mechanisms of the temporal core --- learned restriction
maps (replaced by the identity under the same normalisation), the physical
gap $\Delta_k$ (selector input frozen), and the persistent memory (SSM state
advanced but never read) --- together with the conditioning of the maps
(decoded from the current input, ``current-only''), and we cross every
core variant with the recurrency head switched on or off, because a head
that reads exact-triple recurrence can mask what the core learns.
Table~\ref{tab:factorial} reports the factorial on \textsc{thgl-forum}, the
dataset with the largest temporal-to-spatial ratio in TGB (2.6M
timestamps for 153k nodes; 227 active timestamps per node).

Two regimes emerge. With the recurrency head on, every core variant lies
within seed noise of the full model (two-seed means 0.629--0.641 against a
seed standard deviation of 0.011), and the head over a pointwise projection
of the inputs already reaches 0.615: the head masks the core. With the head
off, the core is worth $+0.16$ MRR over the same projection
(0.394 vs.\ 0.237), and almost all of it comes from the learned restriction
maps --- replacing them by the identity drops the model to 0.220, below the
core-off floor --- with a smaller contribution from the physical gap
($-0.03$) and none from the persistent memory within the four-epoch budget.
The same pattern holds on the coarse-grained streams with the head on
(\textsc{tkgl-smallpedia}, \textsc{tkgl-polecat}, \textsc{thgl-software},
\textsc{tkgl-icews}: all ablations within noise), and on \textsc{tgbl-wiki}
only $\Delta_k$ shows a seed-consistent effect ($0.726 \pm 0.003$ vs.\
$0.733 \pm 0.003$ over three seeds). The controlled synthetic study
(App.~\ref{app:analytic}) is consistent: the faithful architecture beats the
efficient implementation by $\sim$0.1 MRR in every regime, but its variants
are indistinguishable once a recurrency head is present.
